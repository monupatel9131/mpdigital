How Does the Test Work?

01. Ping

Small requests are sent to measure how quickly the server responds.

02. Download

Data is downloaded and the transferred bytes are measured against elapsed time.

03. Upload

Browser-generated data is uploaded when the endpoint supports browser testing.

04. Jitter

Jitter represents variation between latency measurements.

Understanding Your Internet Speed

Download Speed

How quickly information can be received by your device.

Upload Speed

How quickly information can be sent from your device.

Ping

The response time between your device and a test server.

Jitter

The variation in latency over multiple measurements.

Packet Loss

The percentage of test packets that fail to receive a response.

Mbps

Megabits per second, a common measurement of internet speed.

How to Get a More Accurate Speed Test

📶 Use 5 GHz Wi-Fi

When available, 5 GHz can provide better local Wi-Fi performance.

📍 Move Near Router

A stronger Wi-Fi signal can reduce local wireless limitations.

⛔ Stop Downloads

Close downloads, streaming and bandwidth-heavy applications.

🔌 Try Ethernet

Ethernet generally gives a more consistent connection to the router.

🔁 Test Multiple Times

Run tests at different times to account for changing network conditions.

🛡️ Avoid VPN

Disable VPN if you want to measure your normal connection.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an internet speed test?

It measures network performance such as download, upload and latency.

How accurate is MP Speed Test?

It measures performance from your browser, but results can vary with Wi-Fi, device, ISP routing, congestion and server conditions.

Why is my speed different from my ISP plan?

Your ISP plan is not the only factor. Wi-Fi, router, device, congestion and network routing can affect measured speed.

Why is upload speed slower?

Many internet plans are asymmetric, meaning download capacity is intentionally higher than upload capacity.

What is ping?

Ping is the time needed for a request to receive a response, normally measured in milliseconds.

What is jitter?

Jitter measures variation between latency measurements.

What is packet loss?

Packet loss occurs when transmitted data does not successfully receive a response.

Does Wi-Fi affect results?

Yes. Signal strength, interference, distance and Wi-Fi congestion can affect results.

Does MP Speed Test store my results?

History is stored locally in your browser. This website does not intentionally send your history to a server.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works on modern mobile browsers.

Privacy Notice

MP Speed Test performs network measurements directly from your browser. Recent test history may be saved locally in your browser using localStorage. The website does not intentionally store your speed-test history on its own server. Public IP information may be temporarily requested from an external network-information service when network information is loaded. Third-party services may have their own privacy policies.

Actual results may vary depending on your device, Wi-Fi signal, router, ISP routing, server availability, browser limitations and network congestion.
