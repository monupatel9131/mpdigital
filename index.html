What Is Internet Speed?

Download Speed

How quickly data comes from the internet to your device. Essential for streaming video, loading web pages, and downloading files.

Upload Speed

How quickly data is sent from your device to the internet. Important for video calling, online gaming, and sending files.

Ping / Latency

The approximate round-trip latency between your device/browser and the test endpoint. Lower ping means a more responsive connection.

Mbps vs MB/s

Megabits per second (Mbps) measures network bandwidth speed. Megabytes per second (MB/s) measures file size transfer rate. Note: 8 bits = 1 byte.

Frequently Asked Questions

Internet speed refers to the rate at which data travels from the internet to your device (download speed) or from your device to the internet (upload speed), measured in Megabits per second (Mbps).
Most internet service providers allocate more bandwidth to downloading because typical internet activities (like browsing and streaming) involve receiving significantly more data than sending it.
Browser-based tests measure real transferred bytes against exact high-resolution timers (performance.now()). However, browser overhead, background tabs, Wi-Fi interference, and active extensions can influence measurements.
Upload tests or general speed tests can fail if browser extensions (like ad blockers), firewalls, network proxies, or CORS restrictions block binary POST requests to foreign test servers.
